Platform integrations package invoice creation, customer redirection, and
payment verification for a store or billing system.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="WooCommerce" icon="wordpress" href="/store-integrations/woocommerce">
    Accept Billions payments in Classic Checkout and WooCommerce Checkout Blocks.
  </Card>

  <Card title="BILLmanager" icon="server" href="/store-integrations/billmanager">
    Accept Billions payments through the official BILLmanager 6 module.
  </Card>

  <Card title="WHMCS" icon="server" href="/store-integrations/whmcs">
    Accept Billions payments through the official WHMCS gateway module.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Tilda" icon="cart-shopping" href="/store-integrations/tilda">
    Configure Tilda's Custom Payment Gateway to accept Billions payments.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Existing payment modules may also be connected through the
[Unified Merchant API](/integration/unified-merchant-api). This is a
separate integration path from the official Billions modules.
